🧪 Synthetic test data

Generate a complete fake engagement for the client — built from that client's Pre-Engagement setup: its selected roles (one interview per role, biased to each role's priority dimensions), its industry, and its hypotheses, with seeded contradictions and a governance blind spot. Synthetic interviews are appended to the real engagement without touching your briefing; regenerating replaces them. Falls back to a standard 5-executive set when no briefing exists yet. Takes 1–3 minutes.

All interviews
