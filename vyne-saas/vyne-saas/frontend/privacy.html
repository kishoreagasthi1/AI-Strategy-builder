1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how [Vynora Consulting LLC] ("Vynora," "we") collects and processes personal data through the VYNE™ platform (the "Service"). The Service is used by consulting firms ("Customer," "your firm") and, in turn, by two kinds of individuals: (a) Customer's own consultant users (owner/consultant accounts), and (b) individuals at Customer's clients who are invited to complete an AI-readiness interview ("Interviewees"). Where Customer is the one deciding what Interviewee data to collect and why, Customer acts as the data controller for that data and Vynora acts as a processor — see the Data Processing Agreement.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • To provide the Service: authenticate users, isolate each firm's data from every other firm, run AI-assisted interviews and synthesis, and generate roadmap/solution-design content.
  • To bill Customer's firm for its subscription, and to let Customer bill its own clients for AI usage (client cost-recovery billing), at cost.
  • To secure the Service: audit logging of security-relevant events, rate limiting, multi-factor authentication support, and (if configured by Vynora's operations team) error monitoring of unhandled application errors.
  • To communicate service-related notices, including security and billing notices.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Interviewee responses to train our own models.

4. AI Processing and Sub-processors

Interview content and related engagement data are sent to third-party AI model providers to generate scoring, synthesis, and design content. Depending on Customer's plan and Vynora's configuration, this may include Google (Gemini, via Google AI Studio or Vertex AI), Anthropic (via Google Cloud Vertex AI), and OpenAI. Production traffic is configured to exclude free-tier AI endpoints whose provider terms permit using submitted content for model training; paid/enterprise API tiers are used instead, under those providers' respective data-use terms. A current list of sub-processors is available on request and in the DPA.

5. Where Data Is Stored

Application data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Google Cloud SQL) with row-level security enforced per tenant, so one firm's data is not queryable by another firm's application code even in the event of an application-layer bug. Authentication is handled by Google Identity Platform, with a separate isolated user pool per tenant firm.

6. Data Retention

We retain Customer Data for as long as Customer maintains an active account, plus a limited period after termination to allow export (see the DPA). 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because Interviewee data is typically collected on behalf of Customer (the consulting firm engaged by your organization), requests from Interviewees should generally be directed to that firm first; Vynora will assist Customer in responding as described in the DPA. Consultant/owner account holders may contact us directly using the details below.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including per-tenant database isolation (row-level security), encrypted connections, optional multi-factor authentication, structured audit logging of security-relevant actions, and least-privilege database roles that never bypass tenant isolation. No system is perfectly secure; we encourage Customer to enable MFA for all consultant accounts.
