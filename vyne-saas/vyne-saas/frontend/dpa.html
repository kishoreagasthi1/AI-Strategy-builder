Data Processing Agreement

Draft — not yet in effect. Last updated: [DATE]

1. Parties and Relationship to the Terms of Service

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Terms of Service between [Vynora Consulting LLC] ("Processor," "Vynora") and the consulting firm identified as Customer in its account ("Controller," "Customer"). It applies to personal data Processor processes on Controller's behalf in connection with the Service — most significantly, responses collected from Interviewees at Controller's own clients, who are typically not direct customers of Vynora. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy.

2. Subject Matter and Duration

Processor will process personal data on Controller's behalf for the duration of Controller's subscription to the Service, and thereafter only as necessary to comply with Section 9 (Return or Deletion of Data) or as required by law.

3. Nature and Purpose of Processing

Processor processes personal data to provide the Service: hosting and storing engagement data, running AI-assisted interview, synthesis, scoring, and solution-design generation, providing tenant-isolated storage and access controls, and generating usage/billing records.

4. Categories of Data Subjects and Data

Data subjectsCategories of personal data
Controller's consultant users (owner/consultant accounts)Name, work email, role, activity/audit logs
Interviewees (Controller's clients' personnel)Name, role/title, interview responses (text and, if voice mode is used, transcribed audio), engagement metadata

[Controller should confirm whether any special category data (health, etc.) is ever solicited via interview questions — the Service is not designed to collect special category data, and Controller is responsible for configuring interview content accordingly.]

5. Controller and Processor Obligations

Controller warrants that it has a valid legal basis for the processing of personal data it submits to the Service (including Interviewee responses) and has provided any notices or obtained any consents required under applicable law before inviting an Interviewee. Processor will:

  • process personal data only on Controller's documented instructions (including as necessary to provide the Service), unless required otherwise by law;
  • ensure personnel authorized to process personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including per-tenant database row-level security, encrypted connections, and role-based access controls (see Section 6);
  • assist Controller, to the extent reasonably possible, in responding to data subject requests (access, deletion, correction) and in fulfilling Controller's obligations regarding security, breach notification, and data protection impact assessments where applicable;
  • notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller's data;
  • make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and allow for audits by Controller or an independent auditor on reasonable notice, subject to confidentiality.

6. Security Measures

Processor maintains, at minimum: row-level security enforcing per-tenant data isolation at the database layer (not just the application layer); a least-privilege application database role that cannot bypass tenant isolation; encrypted connections between the application and database and between clients and the Service; multi-factor authentication support for consultant accounts; structured audit logging of security-relevant actions (tenant provisioning, team and client-assignment changes); and no storage of raw AI-provider credentials in the browser.

Processor's infrastructure operates primarily in the United States. If Controller or its Interviewees are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the parties agree that transfers of personal data to the United States will be governed by [the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2: Controller-to-Processor), executed as Annex __ to this DPA, together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable]. This section requires review by counsel familiar with current transfer-mechanism requirements before publication.

9. Return or Deletion of Data

Upon termination of Controller's subscription, Processor will make Customer Data available for export for [30] days, after which Processor will delete or anonymize Customer Data, except to the extent retention is required by law or for legitimate audit-log/security purposes as described in the Privacy Policy.

10. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability set out in the Terms of Service, except where such limitation is unenforceable under applicable data protection law.

11. Order of Precedence

In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service regarding the processing of personal data, this DPA controls.
