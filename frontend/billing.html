Cost by client

Real AI cost (LLM + transcription) attributed to each client from the client selected at login, pass-through at cost with no markup. Owners see every client and an "unattributed" bucket for calls made with no client selected (e.g. cross-client admin work); consultants see only clients assigned to them. Text-to-speech cost is not yet priced in this build and always shows as $0 — see the hint on the Statement tab.

Client statement

Pick a client (and optionally a date range) to generate a line-item statement — export it as CSV for your invoicing system, or print it directly.

Who pays for each client

By default every client's work runs on your API keys, is metered per client, and appears on the statement you send them. A client can instead supply their own key — then the charges land on their account directly and never touch your bill.

Keys are supplied by the client through a one-time link. You never see the key itself, only the last four characters and whether it works — so nothing sensitive passes through your inbox.

Model preference by client

A client may ask for a particular model for their analysis, documents and decks. This reorders the providers you already allow for those tasks — it cannot route their work to a provider your policy excludes, and it never changes who pays. Voice interviews are not listed because live audio runs on Gemini regardless; no other provider here supports it.
